Privacy Policy

ID LOGIX Backup  |  Last updated November 24, 2026

ID LOGIX Backup is an internal tool used by ID LOGIX to store copies of its own server backups in Google Drive. It only works with files it creates itself, and it never sells or shares your data.

Who we are

This application is operated by ID LOGIX. It is used by our team to send automated backups from company servers to a company Google Drive account. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When you authorize the app, Google grants it the drive.file permission. This means it can only see, create, update and delete files and folders that the app itself created. It cannot read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

The app does not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, location or any other Google service.

How the data is used

  • Uploading backup files from our servers to Google Drive.
  • Listing, updating and deleting those backup files to manage retention.
  • Downloading those backup files when we need to restore a server.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, analytics, profiling or training of AI models.

How data is stored

The access token issued by Google is stored in a configuration file on our own servers, which are restricted to authorized ID LOGIX staff. Backup files are stored in Google Drive and are covered by Google’s own security and privacy terms.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties. Data is only disclosed if required by law.

Google API Services compliance

ID LOGIX Backup’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app’s access at any time from your Google Account under Third-party apps & services. Once revoked, the app can no longer reach your Google Drive. Backup files it created stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
